Camera Cast

Stream your camera to any screen

Turn a phone or laptop into a live camera and watch it from any browser. No app and no account. The video goes straight from one device to the other.

Watch a camera

Enter the code shown on the camera device.

  1. Start the camera on the device you want to stream from.
  2. Open the link or scan the QR code on any other device.
  3. Watch live. Video flows directly between the two over WebRTC.

Video and sound are encrypted (DTLS-SRTP) and sent peer-to-peer. The public PeerJS server only introduces the two devices; when no direct path exists, the encrypted stream is relayed through a TURN server. Anyone with the camera code can watch, so share it only with people you trust, and use New code to shut everyone out.
